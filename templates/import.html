Import Donations

Upload CSV/XLSX exports from Stripe, PayPal, Zeffy, Facebook, or offline gifts.

How it will work (Phase 1 target):
  1. Pick the source and upload the export file.
  2. The matching column-map template (docs/column-mappings/) lines up their columns to our standard fields.
  3. Data is normalized — dates to ISO, money to cents, emails/phones cleaned.
  4. The matching ladder finds existing donors (email → name+address → name+phone) or stages new ones.
  5. You review the batch before committing; nothing lands until you approve.
  6. Every gift keeps its import-batch ID so a batch can be rolled back.

The upload-and-map UI is wired next. The demo batches below show how history will read.
